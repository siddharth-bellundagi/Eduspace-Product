Our Pre-Primary Learning Journeys

Nurturing young minds through interactive play, creative celebrations, cognitive activities, and motor skill games.

Celebration

Yellow Day Celebration

Nursery Activity: Puzzle block painting – Pineapple

Yellow Day was celebrated with bright smiles, yellow costumes, and creative pineapple printing! Children enjoyed painting pineapple patterns using puzzle block stamps.

LKG Activity: Paper cut collage – Sun

Children in LKG built a vibrant sun using bright paper cut collages. The process encourages pasting, shapes discrimination, and collage craft coordination.

UKG Activity: Crushed paper roll craft – Corn

UKG children crafted unique ears of corn using crushed yellow paper rolls. This active play supports sensory exploration and pincer muscle coordination.

Yellow Day Cheer!

A colourful day filled with creativity, learning, and lots of yellow cheer! Kids posed with their colorful yellow toys and custom banners.

Sensory Color Recognition Creative Craft
Motor Skills

Peel the Tape Activity

Our little learners enjoyed a fun Peel the Tape activity! By sticking and peeling colorful plastic balls on suspended tape lines, they strengthen their finger muscles, improve their pincer grip, and develop fine motor skills essential for early writing. Learning through play made the activity exciting and engaging!

Pincer Grip Pre-Writing Coordination
Celebration

Blue Day Celebration

Nursery Activity: Created a lovely Raindrop Cloud craft

Our Nursery students celebrated Blue Day by building fluffy cloud outlines and adding dangling raindrops. This tactile activity reinforces shapes and elements of nature.

LKG Activity: Made a colourful Umbrella using rangoli

LKG children decorated umbrellas with brilliant colored powder rangoli. This supports fine patterns recognition and hand precision.

UKG Activity: Crafted a beautiful Jellyfish art activity

UKG students made whimsical blue jellyfish hanging art with dangling tentacles. This helps develop cutting, gluing, and spatial assembly skills.

Vibrant Blue Day Celebration!

Pre-primary children came dressed in beautiful shades of blue. The vibrant celebration helped children explore the colour blue while enhancing their creativity and fine motor skills.

Color Association Art & Expression Fine Motor
Celebration

Father's Day Celebration

Nursery Activity: Created a colourful Handprint Trophy

Nursery students created colorful Handprint Trophies to honor their superhero dads. The handprint design builds tactile connection and pride in creative expression.

LKG Activity: Designed a charming Shirt Greeting Card

LKG learners designed charming Shirt Greeting Cards complete with custom paper collars and neckties. This supports folding practice and paper assembly coordination.

UKG Activity: Made a beautiful Heart-Shaped Greeting Card

UKG children made elegant heart-shaped cards with a personalized message inside, expressing deep love and appreciation for their fathers.

Heartfelt Expressions

Our pre-primary students expressed their gratitude and affection through these thoughtful creations, making the celebration of Father's Day truly memorable.

Social-Emotional Paper Crafting Personal Expression
Creative Arts

Fingerprint Art Activity

Nursery Activity: Made a Bee using fingerprints

Our Nursery students used yellow paint to press finger marks, drawing little wings to create busy bees. This sensory exploration aids early finger control and visual tracking.

LKG Activity: Created a beautiful Flower with fingerprint art

LKG children printed colorful blossoms onto dandelion stems. This art activity helps develop rhythm and hand precision.

UKG Activity: Designed colourful Balloons using fingerprints

UKG students printed a vibrant cluster of balloons held by a cute teddy bear outline. The exercise builds concentration and finger pressure adjustment.

Sensory Art Tactile Play Finger Control
Language & Literacy

Sight Words Game

Our UKG children enthusiastically participated in a fun-filled Sight Words Game to strengthen early reading! Through interactive beetle-card matching and table grids, they recognize and read common words like "my", "us", "this", and "as" quickly and confidently. The activity builds reading confidence, concentration, and vocabulary.

Early Reading Word Recognition Interactive Games
Cognitive & Math

Before & After Numbers

Our young learners had great fun during this active math game! By jumping to numbered spots printed on the floor that come before and after a given key number, children reinforce number sequencing, counting logic, physical coordination, and spatial awareness in a joyful, interactive way.

Kinesthetic Math Number Sequences Active Play
Physical Wellness

Yoga Day

Encouraging physical fitness, calmness, and posture control from a young age! During Yoga Day, pre-primary students practiced basic yoga poses, stretching, and simple breathing routines, helping them improve focus, flexibility, and physical coordination.

Gross Motor Mindfulness Health & Fitness
Cognitive & Math

Sort the Shapes Activity

A playful geometric matching game where children sort triangles, circles, squares, and rectangles into their respective slots. This shapes puzzle exercise develops cognitive problem-solving, visual discrimination, and early mathematical sorting logic.

Geometry Visual Discrimination Problem Solving
Cognitive & Math

Count & Write Activity

Laying mathematical foundations through hands-on objects! Children count colorful plastic beads in a bowl, identify the numerical value, and write it on corresponding cards. This tactile association method cements counting rules and numeric symbol matching.

Count & Value Tactile Math Write practice
Celebration

Welcome Day Activity

Making the transition to school a delightful experience! On Welcome Day, students are received with custom-crafted paper bags, "Welcome back to school" mason jar mockups filled with chocolates, and "Erase your fear" erasers. This friendly welcoming eases anxiety and builds high excitement.

Transition Care Greeting Gifts Social Bonding
Language & Literacy

Fun with Alphabets

Building spelling familiarity and fine finger precision! Children trace their names by pasting colorful dot stickers onto letter outlines. This helps them identify letters, recognize their name spellings, and strengthen pincer coordination.

Spelling Recognition Fingertip pasting Name Familiarity
Cognitive & Math

Match the Pair Activity

A brain-stimulating memory game! Using pairs of beautifully illustrated cards spread out on a green table, children identify matching cards (like two snowmen). This builds pattern discrimination, short-term visual memory, and patience.

Visual Memory Pattern Matching Cognitive Focus
Motor Skills

Make the Ball of Activity

A tactile hand-strength activity! Children wrap layers of clear tape or colored papers around plastic balls to increase their diameter. This exercise coordinates both hands working together (bilateral integration), adjusts grip tension, and enhances overall finger dexterity.

Bilateral Integration Hand Strength Tactile Play
