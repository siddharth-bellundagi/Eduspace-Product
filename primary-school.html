Foundational Learning Stage

Inspiring Curiosity & Mastering Fundamental Concepts

The Primary School stage (Grades 1 to 4) is where children transition from play-based exploratory learning to structured intellectual discovery. At Greenwood Public School, our primary wing provides a warm, stimulating environment where foundational literacy, numeracy, and inquisitive reasoning thrive.

Following the Maharashtra State Board curriculum enriched by interactive pedagogy, our dedicated educators nurture every child's innate potential through hands-on activities, storytelling, conceptual math, environmental observation, and expressive arts.

Academic Framework

Subject Domains & Learning Highlights

A balanced curriculum designed to cultivate linguistic competence, mathematical rigor, and social empathy.

English Language & Phonics

Reading fluency, phonics mastery, creative prose writing, vocabulary expansion, and expressive recitation that build confident communication.

Spelling Bees & Storytelling

Mathematics & Logic

Activity-based mathematics focusing on arithmetic operations, mental math drills, geometry shapes, measurement, and problem-solving reasoning.

Manipulatives & Math Games

Environmental Studies (EVS)

Connecting science and society: learning about plant life, animal ecosystems, civic awareness, community helpers, hygiene, and environmental conservation.

Field Projects & Nature Walks

Marathi & Hindi

Building multilingual proficiency with the state language Marathi and national language Hindi through alphabet practice, poems, and everyday dialogue.

State Board Language Standards

Computer & IT Basics

Early digital literacy in our modern computer lab: keyboard skills, Paint drawing, MS Office fundamentals, and safe digital navigation.

Weekly Computer Lab Practicals
