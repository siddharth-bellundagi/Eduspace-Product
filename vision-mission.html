Vision & Mission

Guiding principles that shape every aspect of our educational philosophy.

Our Vision

At Greenwood Public School we are committed and provide a co-educational teaching environment supporting the child to become a well-rounded, confident, multi-culturally aware individual. We believe in close observational assessment in line with the Greenwood School teaching spirit. We intend that all children should enjoy their learning, achieve their potential and become independent life-long learners.

Nurturing Independent Life-Long Learners

Our Mission

To be the first choice for families in Model Colony who look forward to a well-rounded education for their children. We pride ourselves on providing a friendly environment, where enthusiasm and hard work are the foundations for achieving success. Our mission is to value Excellence, Hard work and Success to strengthen all that we do. Our Mission and Vision are shaped by our commitment to educate children.

We will continue with increasing efforts to mould young minds into well balanced personalities with confidence and zeal befitting the standard of this portal of education.

Valuing Excellence, Hard Work & Success
School Motto

“Lead Kindly Light”

Guiding every student towards truth, knowledge, character, and humble service to society.
