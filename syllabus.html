Academic Breakdown

Stage-Wise Academic Scope

A comprehensive view of subjects, instructional focus, and evaluation criteria across all school stages.

Pre-Primary Stage (Nursery, Jr. KG, Sr. KG)

Foundational Play-Way & Early Literacy Development

Ages 2.5 – 5.5 Years
Subject / Area Core Topics & Focus Methodology
English Language & Phonics Alphabet recognition, phonetic sounds, three-letter blending, picture talk, nursery rhymes. Flashcards, storytelling & phonics audio tools
Pre-Number Concepts & Math Numbers 1 to 50, counting with beads, big/small, heavy/light concepts, simple addition concepts. Tactile manipulatives & Montessori apparatus
General Awareness & EVS Myself, family, body parts, seasons, fruits, animals, road safety, and personal hygiene. Theme days, nature walks & visual charts
Creative & Motor Development Clay modelling, paper tearing, fingerprinting, tracing, yoga, and gross motor agility play. Kinesthetic play & art workshops

Primary Stage (Grades 1–4)

Continuous Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE) Aligned

Grades 1 to 4
Subject Key Academic Competencies Assessment Framework
English (First Language) Grammar, prose comprehension, paragraph composition, spelling mastery, reading aloud. Formative oral reading + Written Unit Tests
Mathematics 4 basic arithmetic operations, tables up to 20, fractions, measurement (length, weight), time. Classroom quizzes, worksheets & term exams
Environmental Studies (EVS) Plants, animal life, ecosystems, community services, historical stories, natural resources. Project files, observations & semester tests
Marathi & Hindi Vowels, consonants, matras, sentence construction, simple poems and conversation. Dictation, reading tests & written papers
Computer Basics Hardware components, MS Paint, typing skills, introduction to word processing. Weekly hands-on lab practicals

Secondary Stage (Grades 5–10 SSC)

Maharashtra State Board Board Examination Standard

Class 10 SSC Board
Subject Stream Curriculum Scope Class 10 Board Weightage
Science & Technology (Part 1 & 2) Gravitation, Periodic Table, Chemical Reactions, Electric Current, Life Processes, Heredity & Ecology. 80 Marks Theory + 20 Marks Practical Journal
Mathematics (Algebra & Geometry) Linear Equations, Quadratic Equations, Arithmetic Progression, Similarity, Circle Theorems, Trigonometry. 80 Marks Theory + 20 Marks Internal Assessments
Social Sciences History & Applied History, Political Science & Constitution, Geography & Economics. 80 Marks Theory + 20 Marks Internal Evaluation
First Language: English Prose, Poetry, Rapid Reading, Unseen Comprehension, Expansion of Ideas, Letter Writing, Report Drafting. 80 Marks Written + 20 Marks Oral / Listening
Second & Third Languages Marathi (Higher/Lower Level) and Hindi Lokbharati / Composite Sanskrit options. 80 Marks Written + 20 Marks Oral / Listening
Evaluation System

Evaluation & Assessment Structure

Consistent, transparent evaluation measuring academic progress, concept application, and co-curricular skills.

Formative Continuous Evaluation

Classroom quizzes, laboratory experiments, oral presentations, homework projects, and disciplined classroom conduct throughout the academic terms.

Summative Examinations

Two comprehensive Unit Tests and two Semester Term Examinations per year, ensuring students master the syllabus incrementally and thoroughly.

Parent-Teacher Dialogues

Regular PTMs after each examination where teachers share detailed performance reviews, individualized study advice, and attendance updates with parents.
