Experiential Pedagogy

Learning Beyond the Four Walls of Classrooms

Education achieves its highest impact when students encounter concepts firsthand. At Greenwood Public School, educational excursions and annual field trips are thoughtfully integrated into our academic roadmap to cultivate curiosity, observation, and respect for nature and heritage.

From astronomical observatories and interactive science centres across Pune to historical monuments and agro-nature trails, our tours spark wonder, forge lifelong camaraderie, and teach independence in a safe, mentored environment.

Expedition Pillars

Curated Excursion Domains

Structured grade-appropriate outings designed around specific academic and developmental goals.

Science & Innovation Trips

Grades 4 through 10

Interactive visits to premier scientific institutions where students witness physics laws, mechanical automation, and astronomical wonders in action.

  • Pimpri Chinchwad Science Park
  • Planetarium & Sky Observation
  • Science Expo Demonstrations

Historical & Heritage Trails

Grades 3 through 10

Immersion in Maharashtra’s glorious history, architectural mastery, and freedom struggle through guided heritage walks around Pune cantonment and landmarks.

  • Sinhagad Fort & Maratha Architecture
  • Raja Dinkar Kelkar Museum
  • Aga Khan Palace Heritage Site

Ecology, Farms & Annual Picnic

Pre-Primary to Grade 10

Refreshing days in nature designed for joyful play, agricultural learning, understanding flora and fauna, and building lifelong bonds between teachers and peers.

  • Empress Botanical Garden Flora Walks
  • Agro-Tourism & Organic Farm Visits
  • Annual School Picnic & Fun Parks
Safety Assurance

Our Safety & Chaperone Standards

Every excursion adheres to strict regulatory compliance, parental communication, and medical preparedness.

Dedicated Faculty Escorts

Every group is escorted by class teachers and attendants with strict 1:10 supervision.

Comprehensive First Aid

Certified medical kits and emergency contacts accompany every school tour vehicle.

Certified RTO Transport

Only registered luxury coaches with speed governors and background-checked drivers are contracted.

Parent Updates & Consent

Written parental consent is mandatory, accompanied by real-time trip coordination updates.
