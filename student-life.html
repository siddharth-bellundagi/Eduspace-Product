Student Life & Activities

Home / Student Life

Nurturing Character, Creativity & Confidence

At Greenwood Public School, student life is designed to ignite curiosity, encourage collaboration, and celebrate every child's unique gifts. From competitive athletic matches on our grounds to creative arts, pre-primary wonder, and experiential educational journeys, our campus offers a lively world of opportunity.

Sports & Athletics

Physical Fitness, Agility & Team Spirit

Our structured physical education curriculum instills stamina, resilience, and fair play through football, cricket, track events, and our spirited four-house championship.

  • 4-House League (Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, Topaz)
  • Daily Morning Physical Fitness & Yoga Sessions
  • Annual Grand Sports Day Championship

Cultural Activities

Performing Arts, Music, Exhibitions & Festivities

A vibrant stage for elocution, music, dramatics, school choir, and our celebrated Annual Science and Art Exhibition that showcases student innovation.

  • Annual Science & Art Exhibition Projects
  • Patriotic & National Festival Celebrations
  • Elocution, Debating & Theatrical Skits
Explore Cultural Activities

Pre-Primary Activities

Joyful Experiential Learning for Little Stars

Play-based learning in our colourful kindergarten: tactile crafts, sensory motor exercises, celebration of special colour days, and foundational joy.

  • 80+ Photo Chronicle of Pre-Primary Celebrations
  • Sensory Play, Clay Modelling & Origami
  • Kinesthetic Math & Early Literacy Exploration
Explore Pre-Primary Activities

Educational Trips

Experiential Outings, Heritage & Nature Discovery

Curriculum-linked excursions across Pune: science centers, historical forts, botanical nature walks, and annual picnics under 1:10 faculty supervision.

  • Science Park & Planetarium Excursions
  • Historical Heritage & Fort Expeditions
  • Comprehensive Safety & Medical Chaperone Protocols
