Maharashtra State Board (SSC)

Academic Rigor, Scientific Inquiry & Board Mastery

The Secondary School wing (Grades 5 to 10) at Greenwood Public School is dedicated to fostering analytical depth, disciplined habits, and comprehensive academic preparedness under the Maharashtra SSC Board (Pune Division).

With well-equipped science laboratories, computer training centres, structured remedial coaching, and continuous practice via preliminary mock exams, we empower students to achieve standout results in their Class 10 Board Examinations while cultivating ethical leadership.

SSC Curriculum

Key Secondary Disciplines

Structured preparation aligned with Maharashtra State Board directives for conceptual excellence.

Science & Technology (I & II)

Rigorous physics, chemistry, and life sciences curricula backed by regular laboratory practicals, journal evaluations, and scientific model exhibitions.

Weekly Laboratory Practicals

Mathematics (Algebra & Geometry)

Mastery in algebraic formulas, quadratic equations, coordinate geometry, trigonometry, and statistics through systematic step-by-step problem sets.

Special Doubt-Clearing Tutorials

Social Sciences

Deep historical understanding of modern India and world civilizations, Indian political processes and constitution, economics, and physical geography.

Map Work & Historical Seminars

Languages (English, Marathi & Hindi)

High-level linguistic proficiency in English First Language, along with Marathi and Hindi. Emphasis on essay writing, letter drafting, and comprehension skills.

Grammar & Writing Workshops

Information Technology (ICT)

Comprehensive computer science training: programming basics, web fundamentals (HTML/CSS), spreadsheet management, and cyber security ethics.

1:1 Computer Lab Workstations

Health, PE & Social Service

Mandatory physical education graded according to State Board benchmarks, yoga, disaster management training, and community service projects.

State Board Graded Subjects
Strategic Mentoring

Our 4-Step SSC Board Coaching Model

Empowering every student to face the Class 10 Board Examinations with self-assurance.

01. Syllabus Completion

Complete coverage of the entire SSC Board syllabus by November to allow ample revision.

02. Prelims & Mock Tests

Two full-length preliminary examinations mirroring strict SSC Board time-limits and grading patterns.

03. Remedial Tutorials

Specialized after-school remedial sessions focused on difficult theorems, numericals, and grammar.

04. Wellness & Guidance

Stress-management workshops, time management counselling, and dietary advice for exam days.
