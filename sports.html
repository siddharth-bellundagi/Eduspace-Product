Physical Education & Games

Building Physical Agility, Team Spirit & Endurance

At Greenwood Public School, sports and physical education are fundamental pillars of our holistic educational curriculum. We believe that an active, healthy body fuels a sharp, focused intellect. Our sports training is designed to instill lifelong qualities of teamwork, sportsmanship, resilience, self-discipline, and leadership in every student.

Guided by certified physical education instructors, our students engage in structured coaching across multiple outdoor and indoor sports disciplines, track and field athletics, martial arts (Taekwondo/Karate), and daily yoga exercises.

Our Sporting Disciplines

Comprehensive training programs nurturing budding athletes across multiple sports.

Track & Field Athletics

Sprints (100m, 200m, 400m), relay races, long jump, shot put, and cross-country fitness conditioning under expert guidance.

Team Sports & Football

Football, volleyball, dodgeball, and basketball fostering tactical thinking, communication, camaraderie, and team spirit.

Martial Arts & Self-Defense

Taekwondo, karate, and judo classes designed to build mental fortitude, reflexes, self-defense skills, and core physical strength.

Yoga & Mindfulness

Daily morning asanas, pranayama breathing, and posture exercises enhancing concentration, flexibility, and emotional calmness.

Badminton & Table Tennis

Fast-paced racquet sports developing hand-eye coordination, quick decision making, agility, and stamina on marked courts.

Chess & Strategic Games

Indoor mind sports sharpening spatial reasoning, patience, logical foresight, and strategic problem-solving abilities.

The Four Houses

Instilling healthy competitive spirit, house loyalty, and peer leadership.

Red House

Courage & Valor

Embodying determination, energy, and relentless pursuit of excellence in all sports tournaments.

Blue House

Integrity & Loyalty

Striving for consistent performance, sportsmanship, and mutual support across track and field.

Green House

Harmony & Growth

Symbolizing perseverance, continuous improvement, and dynamic coordination in team events.

Yellow House

Enthusiasm & Brilliance

Radiating optimism, sports brilliance, and high spirit during inter-house championships.
