From the Principal's Desk

Leading with love, knowledge, purpose, and integrity.

Mrs. Reena Mathew

Principal

Greenwood Public School, Pune
“Lead Kindly Light”
Official Address

I am delighted to introduce our school to you through this website and to offer you a glimpse into the rich legacy of excellence that our school has built over the years. For nearly four decades we have remained steadfast in our commitment to our motto, “Lead Kindly Light” by nurturing responsible individuals who are well equipped to face the challenges of an ever-changing world as well as strive to make a positive difference in the spaces they are a part of – at home, in school, and in society.

Beyond being a hub for academic, cultural, literary, artistic, and sporting activities that enable our students to discover their talents, our school has left no stone unturned to develop individuals of strong character. We believe in providing an environment where every student's innate potential is identified, valued, and encouraged.

I take this opportunity to thank and appreciate all our stakeholders, including the Management, Administrator, Staff and Parents who continue to be the backbone of our institution. Their relentless support, trust, and collaboration are the true pillars of our success.

“With love as our guide and knowledge as our light, we nurture young minds to lead with kindness, purpose, and integrity.”

As we stride forward into new horizons of modern education, our focus remains firmly centered on imparting values that endure for a lifetime. We invite you to be part of our inspiring educational journey.

Mrs. Reena Mathew Principal, Greenwood Public School
