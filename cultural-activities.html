Creative Expression & Heritage

Nurturing Artistic Talents & Cultural Harmony

At Greenwood Public School, education transcends textbooks and examination papers. We believe that true growth happens when students discover their voice, express their creativity, and celebrate India’s diverse cultural mosaic with joy and reverence.

Through our rich tapestry of musical performances, theatrical productions, science and arts exhibitions, debates, and festival celebrations, every student is encouraged to step onto the stage, build self-confidence, and embrace aesthetic appreciation.

Creative Spectrum

Four Dimensions of Cultural Growth

Providing multifaceted platforms where every child finds their creative spark and flourishes.

Music & Choral Arts

Vocal training, classical ragas, school choir hymns, and instrumental appreciation that instill rhythm, discipline, and emotional poise.

Fine Arts & Crafts

Painting, sketching, origami, clay modelling, and eco-friendly craft workshops that stimulate spatial awareness and fine motor coordination.

Elocution & Drama

English and Marathi debate, extempore speaking, poetry recitation, and dramatic skits that nurture articulate leaders and stage presence.

Annual Exhibitions

A signature school event where students present working scientific models, historical dioramas, and creative artwork to parents and guests.

Festive Calendar

Signature Annual Celebrations

Traditions and events that bring our students, teachers, and parent community together.

AUG 15th

Independence Day & Investiture Ceremony

Solemn tricolor flag unfurling, march-past parade, patriotic choral presentations, and the formal induction of the Student Prefectorial Council.

SEP 5th

Teachers' Day Gratitude Assembly

Senior students assume the mantle of educators for the day, organizing heartwarming musical dedications and cultural skits in honour of their mentors.

DEC Annual

Annual Science & Art Exhibition

A school-wide carnival of young innovators displaying working physics models, environmental conservation exhibits, and intricate artistic canvases.

JAN 26th

Republic Day & Cultural Fest

Celebrations reflecting the Constitutional values of equality and fraternity, accompanied by vibrant regional folk dances representing diverse Indian states.
