About Our School

Home / About Us
Co-Educational English Medium School

Nurturing Minds, Building Characters Since 1989

I welcome you through our website to Greenwood Public School, a co–educational English Medium School, managed by a Public Trust under the chairmanship of the Education Trust Chairman. Every student is unique and we provide them with the opportunities to fructify their potential and build their self confidence so that they can face new challenges and grow into confident individuals. We have staff committed to providing the best for every child. Thus every child progresses academically as well as develops their own unique talents.
