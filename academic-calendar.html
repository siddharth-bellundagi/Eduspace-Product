Annual Framework

Term Breakdown & Major Vacations

Key academic milestones structured across First and Second Terms.

First Term (June – October)

School Reopening, Investiture & Semester 1 Assessments

Mid June
School Reopening & Welcome Assembly

Classes commence for Pre-Primary, Primary and Secondary wings.

Early August
Unit Test 1 (All Classes)

First formal assessment testing opening quarter competencies.

August 15
Independence Day & Investiture Ceremony

Tricolor hoisting, parade, and student council badge decoration.

Mid October
Semester 1 Term Exams & Report Cards

Summative evaluation followed by Parent-Teacher Conference.

Late Oct – Nov
Diwali Vacations (approx. 20 Days)

Festive recess for students and faculty.

Second Term (November – April)

Sports Day, Annual Day, SSC Prelims & Annual Exams

Mid November
School Reopens for Term 2 & Children's Day

Celebration of Children's Day with student fairs and fun activities.

December
Annual Sports Meet & Science Exhibition

Four-house track and field athletic championship and innovation expo.

January
Unit Test 2 & Class 10 Prelims 1

Second unit tests for grades 1–9 and full board simulation for SSC.

March – April
SSC Board Exams & Annual Final Exams

Maharashtra State Board SSC exams and school-wide final assessments.

May 1 Onward
Maharashtra Day & Summer Vacation

Annual results declaration followed by summer break.

Examination Protocol

General Guidelines for Students

Ensuring fairness, integrity, and discipline during all school and board assessments.

Punctuality

Students must be seated in their allotted examination rooms at least 15 minutes before the commencement of the exam.

Hall Tickets & ID

Official school identity card and exam admit card (where applicable) are strictly mandatory for entry into the exam hall.

75% Attendance

A minimum of 75% classroom attendance across the academic terms is mandatory to be eligible to appear for final examinations.

Medical Absence

In case of illness during exam days, an official medical certificate must be submitted to the Principal within 48 hours.
