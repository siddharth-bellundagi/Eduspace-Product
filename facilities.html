World-Class Facilities

Our campus is equipped with modern infrastructure designed to provide a safe, enriched, and positive learning environment for every student.

Smart Classrooms

Technology-integrated classrooms with digital boards and multimedia support for interactive and engaging learning experiences.

Library

A vast collection of children's books, fiction, reference volumes, and academic publications promoting active reading habits among students.

Computer Lab

State-of-the-art computing units providing hands-on digital literacy training and programming fundamentals for students.

Science Lab

Well-equipped science laboratory with modern instruments and apparatus for conducting practical experiments in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.

Kids Play Area

A safe and colourful outdoor play area designed specifically for Pre-Primary and younger students to develop motor skills through play.

CCTV Surveillance

24/7 CCTV surveillance coverage across classrooms, corridors, and outdoor areas ensuring comprehensive monitoring and student safety.

Safety & Security

Managed gates, trained security personnel, fire safety equipment, and strict visitor protocols ensuring a secure campus environment.
