Founded in 1989

A Timeless Legacy in Historic Model Colony

Nestled in the historic cantonment town of Model Colony, along the banks of the Mula River near Pune, Maharashtra, Greenwood Public School stands as a testament to a legacy built on faith, knowledge, discipline and service.

Model Colony holds a distinguished place in Indian history as the site of the famous Battle of Model Colony in 1817. Following the battle, it developed into a major military cantonment and over the years, emerged as an important defence hub, home to significant military establishments, defence facilities and research institutions. Enriched by its military heritage and diverse community, Model Colony embodies the values of discipline, dedication, and a spirit of service.

It was in this inspiring environment that our visionary founder, the Late Dr. Robert Williams, envisioned an educational institution committed to academic excellence and the holistic development of young minds. His dream was to make quality English-medium education accessible to the children of defence personnel, railway employees and the wider civilian community of Pune, creating opportunities for generations to learn, grow and succeed.

Founded in 1989, Greenwood Public School was founded with a vision to bring holistic, value-based English medium education to the community. Guided by the enduring principles of faith, integrity, compassion and perseverance, the school began its journey with humble beginnings and a steadfast commitment to meaningful education.

A Spiritual Leader and Guiding Light

Mr. David Fernandes, Bishop of the Regional Education Council of the Education Trust, is a respected spiritual leader with a strong academic background in arts, theology and pastoral studies. Dedicated to School Trust service, Christian education and student welfare, he brings extensive pastoral experience and has contributed significantly to the growth of the Regional Education Council.

His spiritual leadership and blessings continue to guide Greenwood Public School in its mission of providing quality education and nurturing strong values.

A Vision Strengthened by Dedicated Leadership

Late Dr. A. K. Mehta was the Director of Greenwood Education Trust, Pune, Managing Trustee and Director of Greenwood Secondary School, Pune, and a distinguished legal academic, Attorney at Law, author, and social worker. He also served as Director of the National Academy of Continuing Legal Education and the Regional Education Board.

A visionary educationist, he dedicated his life to provide quality education to the weaker sections of society. His dream was to establish Greenwood Public School as a centre of excellence, and his guidance and commitment contributed immensely to its growth and success.

His outstanding contribution to legal education included numerous publications, 19 volumes of the Manual of Maharashtra Laws, and the textbook Legislative Principles and Judicial Process. He guided eight PhD scholars in law and received prestigious awards, including the IALS Lifetime Achievement Award for Excellence in Legal and Justice Education and the Global Law Dean's Forum Award 2017.

His visionary leadership, remarkable scholarship and lifelong dedication to education and society will always be remembered with deep respect and gratitude.

A Legacy That Inspires the Future

Affiliated with the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education, Pune, Greenwood Public School continues to uphold the ideals upon which it was established. Through its commitment to academic achievement, character formation and the all-round development of every student, the school strives to nurture confident, responsible and compassionate individuals.

From the founding vision of Dr. Robert Williams to the dedicated leadership and invaluable contributions of Late Dr. A. K. Mehta, the school's journey reflects a shared belief that education is not merely the pursuit of knowledge, but the foundation of character, opportunity and service to humanity.

Today, Greenwood Public School carries this distinguished legacy forward with pride and purpose — empowering young minds with knowledge, grounding them in enduring values and inspiring them to become responsible citizens and leaders of tomorrow.

“Our legacy is measured not merely by the years behind us, but by the lives we shape, the dreams we nurture and the brighter future we strive to create.”
